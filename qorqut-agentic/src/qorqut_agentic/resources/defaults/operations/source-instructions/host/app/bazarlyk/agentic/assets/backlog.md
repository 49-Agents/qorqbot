The backlog is snoozed tasks: when the owner says backlog, they mean snoozed. Add to the backlog by setting snoozed_until (one shared timezone-aware future timestamp) on tasks_create or tasks_update, review it with snooze_filter="snoozed", and set changes.snoozed_until to null to take a task out. Task snoozing defers the shared due reminder, not status, due dates, or dependencies, and the Task wakes once when its time passes. tasks_list defaults to snooze_filter="active"; use "snoozed" or "all" to review deferred work. Do not snooze urgent blockers just to clear the queue.
