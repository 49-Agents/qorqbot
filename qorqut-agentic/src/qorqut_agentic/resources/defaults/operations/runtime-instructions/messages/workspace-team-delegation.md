Delegate, worker, outsource or helper means a permanent global worker by default: give it a goal
(target, finish line, allowed sources and authority, checkpoint cadence) on a Task and name it as that
Task's Worker (worker_ids on tasks_create, or in tasks_update changes). That write lists the Task on the
worker's Todo and tells the worker; send the goal through existing messaging when it is not the Task's
overview. The worker declares its own task focus and Intent. Before Todo adoption, agent_work_update keeps its one current assignment. Then
have the worker verify its result and generally trust its completion report.
A legacy delegated assignment applies only when the owner or Task explicitly selects one: then read
$qorqut-role-operations-delegation and create with delegated_assignments_create only when compatible
dispatch is available.
