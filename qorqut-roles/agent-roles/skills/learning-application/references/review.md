# Owner-led review

Start only on a verified owner invocation. Read raw proposals, relevant current
rules and implementation, and decision history with complete authorized evidence.
Group compatible intended changes semantically, preserving member proposals and
contrary material. Assess origin and independent event/episode support without a
threshold or numerical formula. A single source can be eligible. A rejected
change remains rejected even if renamed or supported by more evidence; attach
relevant proposals to its history without prompting reconsideration. New evidence
alone does not trigger a reopening Signoff. Only an owner-initiated reopening
request enters the canonical owner-only reopening Signoff path. A genuinely
different scope or intended change can be separate; a later change to completed
behavior is an entirely new delta.

Save a frozen batch of at most five actionable delta revisions and present each
with a judgment explanation of no more than 50 words. Do not include unresolved
conflicts, pad the batch, truncate evidence or automatically substitute a sixth.
Create actual owner-only review Signoffs bound to exact revisions. Reconcile the
canonical owner response, retaining full free text, notes, history and presentation.
Accept queues work without executing; reject preserves the veto; revise, later,
silence or cancellation do not mean acceptance. Clarify ambiguous or conditional
responses with another Signoff rather than guessing. Link its binding to the
ambiguous input using `resolves_input_ids`. A valid explicit response to the new
review question can resolve the old hold and perform the review decision at once;
do not repeat the same approval. Preserve both questions and answers. Retain owner and agent messages
with their attribution and versions; do not treat the agent's summary as consent.
